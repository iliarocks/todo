# Nagare

A quiet place to plan today, look ahead, and stay pointed at what matters.

## Features

- *Today* — what you're doing right now
- *Upcoming* — what's on the horizon
- *Vision* — the long view, revisited on your own schedule

Plus repeating items, timed events, projects, and rich notes.

## Concepts

- *Item* — a single todo or event on a specific date
- *Template* — a recurring item; generates instances on its schedule
- *Group* — a project (active) or bucket (archived) you can attach items to
- *Vision* — a long-form text representing your direction, with an optional horizon date

## Install as a PWA

Nagare works best installed to your home screen — it launches fullscreen, hides the browser chrome, and feels native.

*iOS / iPadOS (Safari)*
1. Open Nagare in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Scroll down and tap *Add to Home Screen*
4. Tap *Add*

*macOS (Safari 17+)*
1. Open Nagare in Safari
2. From the menu bar, choose *File → Add to Dock*

*Android (Chrome)*
1. Open Nagare in Chrome
2. Tap the three-dot menu
3. Tap *Add to Home screen* or *Install app*

*Windows / Desktop (Chrome, Edge)*
1. Open Nagare in your browser
2. Click the install icon in the address bar
3. Or open the menu and choose *Install Nagare* / *Apps → Install this site*
